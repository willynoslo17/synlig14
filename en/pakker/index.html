Frequently asked questions

What do you need from me?

Logo, images, draft text or keywords, contact information and access to the domain. You get two feedback rounds.

What happens if I don't have text or images?

I agree that with you in the offer. I can help with draft text created with AI that you approve and you can use the images you have.

What does "ready for AI search" mean?

Structured data, FAQ, llms.txt and clear text that make it easier for Google and AI services to understand your business. Read more at Ready for AI search.

Can I change the page myself?

The site is static and fast. I can make small changes via Maintenance. Major changes are agreed separately.

Why Norwegian and Spanish?

Because many companies in Norway have Spanish-speaking customers or owners. English can be added later.
