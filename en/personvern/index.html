What information is collected

When you use the contact form, I can receive: name, company, e-mail, telephone, current website, message, selected package and that you have given consent. A hidden field (honeypot) is also used against spam - it must be left empty.

Purpose and legal basis

The information is only used to respond to your inquiry and follow up on any agreement. The legal basis is consent (GDPR art. 6 (1) letter a / Personal Data Act).

Storage time

If there is no customer relationship, the inquiry will be deleted after 12 months at the latest. In the event of an agreement, information is stored for as long as is necessary for the agreement and statutory duties (for example accounting).

Data processors

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. If a webhook is configured for the contact form, that service (such as an automation tool) also receives the same information for forwarding to me. No tracking cookies or analysis tools are used on this website.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability and withdraw consent. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).

Cookies on this page

This website does not use cookies for tracking or analysis.

Websites delivered to customers

On websites I create for customers where tracking has been agreed, pixels and similar tools are only activated after consent (cookie banner). The customer is responsible for processing personal data on their own website.
