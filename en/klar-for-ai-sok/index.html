Ready for AI search

Make it easier for Google and AI to understand your business

More are asking ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity instead of just searching Google. These services read web pages to respond. Synlig14 builds a foundation that makes it easier for them to understand what you're doing.

I do this on every website

  • Clear text about what you do, where and for whom.
  • Structured data (Schema.org) for company, services, opening hours and FAQ.
  • Frequently asked questions written as people actually ask.

Questions and answers

What does "ready for AI search" mean?

That the website has clear text, structured data, FAQ, llms.txt and a fast mobile page, so that Google and AI services can more easily understand what the company does.

Can someone promise that ChatGPT mentions my business?

No. No one can promise that. What I can do is make it as easy as possible for them to understand who you are and what you offer.

What is an AI visibility check?

A test of 10–15 typical customer questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity in Norwegian, English, Swedish, Polish or other languages, with report, screenshots and a prioritized action list.

Do AI responses change over time?

Yes. The report is a snapshot of the moment, because the responses from AI services are changing.
